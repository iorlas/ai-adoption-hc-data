# Game — which tool

**▸ Together, out loud. Five minutes, no laptops.**

Five questions someone might actually ask on a Tuesday. For each: **Genie,
Claude Code, or neither** — and one sentence for why.

Answer before the person next to you. Your reason matters more than your pick.

---

**1.** "How many donors registered in Manchester last quarter?"

**2.** "Why did the donor count drop by twenty last week?"

**3.** "What does the `consent_research` column actually mean?"

**4.** "Rewrite this 400-line notebook so we can reuse the profiling bits."

**5.** "Which of our fourteen Mapping Data Flows can be converted to views?"

---

## The one to argue about

Number 3. It looks like a Genie question and it is the one Genie is least able
to answer honestly.
