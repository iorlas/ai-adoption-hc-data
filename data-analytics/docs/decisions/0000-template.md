## How anyone would know this was applied

_The part most decision records miss. If someone reads a number produced under
this decision, what tells them the decision was in force? A note on the report?
A comment in the measure? Nothing at all?_

_"Nothing at all" is a legitimate answer and worth writing down, because it is
the thing that bites in eighteen months._
