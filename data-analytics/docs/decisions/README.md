# Decisions

One numbered file per decision, never edited once accepted — superseded by a
later one instead.

## Why bother

Two reasons, and the second is the one that surprises people.

**For your colleagues:** it stops the same argument happening every six months.
The "active supporters" email thread has been running since March precisely
because the decision was never written down anywhere it could be pointed at.

**For Claude:** a decision record is the only thing that stops it re-proposing
the option you already rejected. Without it, every fresh conversation starts
from the same blank slate you did.

And a third, which is really Lucie's:

> *"I need to be able to stand up in front of my stakeholder and explain how it
> works and how the numbers are derived."*

A decision record is that explanation, written down once, in advance, by the
people who actually made the call.

## The shape

Copy `0000-template.md`. The one part not to skip is **the options you
rejected** — a decision without its rejected alternatives is just an assertion,
and it will be re-litigated.
