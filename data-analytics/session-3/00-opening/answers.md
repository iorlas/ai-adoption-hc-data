## What goes wrong

**Someone is IT-blocked.** Pair them immediately, take their name, and follow up
after — do not spend session time on it. Three people on the other stream lost
two whole days to this in July.

**The room wants to relitigate July.** Acknowledge in one sentence and move:
*"that's fair, and it's why today is shaped the way it is."*

**You over-run.** Cut the July recap to a single sentence. Everything else here
is load-bearing: the "what the August call changed" beat is what buys the day,
and the green check is what stops an hour disappearing later.
