# If you were not here on Monday

Two of Tuesday's parts assume you have files that Session 3 produced:

| Tuesday part | Expects | Made in |
|---|---|---|
| `03-adf-weak-spots/`, step 3 | `docs/data-quality-rules.md` | Session 3, part 1 |
| `04-better-questions/` | A Definitions section in `CLAUDE.md` | Session 3, part 3 |

If you missed Monday, joined late, or your files got mangled — take the two in
this folder and carry on. Nothing in Tuesday is blocked.

## How to use them

1. Copy `data-quality-rules.md` over the top of `docs/data-quality-rules.md`.
2. Open `claude-md-definitions.md`, copy the block, and paste it into your
   `CLAUDE.md` in place of the `_TODO_` under Definitions.

Two minutes. Then everything on Tuesday works normally.

## What you are missing, honestly

These are the answers, and reading an answer is not the same as arriving at one.
What Monday actually taught was **the argument**: four defensible numbers, none
of them wrong, and a room deciding which question they were being asked. That
does not fit in a file.

If you want it, the two exercises are written to be followed alone —
`session-3/02-data-quality/README.md` and
`session-3/04-shared-definitions/README.md`. About an hour, and everything you
need is in the repository. Delete these fallback files first, or you will be
marking your own homework.

## Facilitator note

Hand these out **quietly and individually**, not to the room. Someone who missed
a session should not have to say so in front of both teams to get unblocked.
